# par-store

Wires `par`'s pushed requests: `records`' records in `par-store.pushed`, each for the lifetime `par` gives (the JAR's expiry and the clock skew, minutes at most). The space declares a guarantee for each need `par` states (confirmed reads, replicated, never evicted, authenticated); a need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check. It is `retained`: a JAR's `claims` or `login_hint` may name a subject, kept no longer than the JAR lives and the clock skew, the reservation of its start's lifetime; it needs no subject writer, as nothing in it is found by subject. On a store that does not tell a write made outside the provider apart, it boots only under store's `accept-unauthenticated`. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `par`, `records` and `store`.
