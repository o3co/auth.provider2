Wires `transparency-local`'s store interface, `logStore`, to `store` through `transparency-local`'s `logStoreOver`: each flow's records, written as one by `records`' records held across. Its one space, `transparency-local-store.log`, declares a guarantee for each need `transparency-local` states (never evicted, durable, replicated, confirmed reads, authenticated); a need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check. On a store that does not tell a write made outside the provider apart, the space boots only under store's `accept-unauthenticated`, as store holds every space needing `authenticated`. It refuses the boot, through `logStoreOver` and the kernel's `refuse` naming `transparency-local-store`, over a store with no batch across. The space is `retained`: a flow's records are kept as a log keeps its flows, with no expiry. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `store` and `transparency-local`.
