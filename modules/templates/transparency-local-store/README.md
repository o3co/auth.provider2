Wires `transparency-local`'s store interface, `logStore`, to `store` through `transparency-local`'s `logStoreOver`: each flow's records, written as one by `records`' records held across. Its one space, `transparency-local-store.log`, declares a guarantee for each need `transparency-local` states (never evicted, durable, replicated, confirmed reads, authenticated); a need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check. On a store that does not tell a write made outside the provider apart, the space boots only under store's `accept-unauthenticated`, as store holds every space needing `authenticated`. It refuses the boot, through `logStoreOver` and the kernel's `refuse` naming `transparency-local-store`, over a store with no batch across. The space is `retained`: a flow's records are kept as a log keeps its flows, with no expiry. It also gives `transparency-local`'s judgment its clients (`clientRecords`) as `clients` holds them: a client's registry version, whether signed, the keys written in it (`client-assertion`'s section) and its own ceiling (`client-allowance`'s `allowedSelfIssuance` at `issuer`'s URL). The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `store`, `transparency-local`, `clients`, `client-assertion`, `client-allowance` and `issuer`.
