# pushed

Unit of `attack-harness`'s internal module `front-steps`: the steps of a login's request pushed (PAR, RFC 9126).

## Responsibilities
- A PAR login through the real SDK (§3.3): the screen reached by `client_id` and `request_uri` alone, holding no JAR, reading T by `readPushed`; the compromised Front's swaps of the request inside ② (the screen refuses) and at the URL (the relying party's R3 refuses).
- A Core taking pushed requests alone (`par`'s `pushed-only`, FAPI 2.0's F4): its metadata says so, a JAR by value is refused at ② with its word and cause, a JAR started pushed and resent by value under the same screen keys among them, and a login pushed completes.

## Not here
- What every step holds, and their world: its module's card.

## Offers
- Nothing to import: its tests are the steps.

## Invariants
- A Core per file: the default pattern's, by value taken beside pushed; one taking pushed requests alone.

## Depends on
- Its module's world; the package's `pattern` (its start's limit) and `roles` (the screen, the relying party).
