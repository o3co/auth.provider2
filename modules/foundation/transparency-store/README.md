Wires `transparency`'s frame store interface, `frameStore`, to `store` through `transparency-frames`' `frameStoreOver`: a unit's frame and its parts' records, written as one by `records`' records held across. Its one space, `transparency-store.frames`, declares a guarantee for each need `transparency` states (never evicted, durable, replicated, confirmed reads, authenticated); a need with nothing mapped to it fails the type check. On a store that does not tell a write made outside the provider apart, the space boots only under store's `accept-unauthenticated`, as store holds every space needing `authenticated`. It refuses the boot, through `frameStoreOver` and the kernel's `refuse` naming `transparency-store`, over a store with no batch across, or where a part's place another wiring binds (`frameParts`) declares other guarantees than the frames, or holds a subject's data. The space is `retained`: a frame is kept as the log keeps its flow, with no expiry, but a client's op's, which `transparency` drops once its op ends; it also holds each client's flow's record: its open ops, and its ops closed with nothing on the log, kept as long as the space. The wiring only maps, with no branches or loops. Depends on `kernel`, `store`, `transparency` and `transparency-frames`.
